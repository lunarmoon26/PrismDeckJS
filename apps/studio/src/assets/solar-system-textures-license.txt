The maps are derived from textures published by Solar System Scope and based on
NASA imagery data. CyberHUD resized overview maps for its Galaxy Web Applet and
retains source-sized detail maps. PrismDeckJS converted overview and star maps to WebP and
uses 1024 x 512 WebP derivatives for Sol, Earth, Luna, Jupiter, and Saturn hero
views plus a 2048 x 1024 solar-sky map. No endorsement by Solar System Scope or
NASA is implied.
